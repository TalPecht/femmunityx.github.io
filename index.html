OUR RESEARCH VISION

Across states.

THE DIRECTION

Towards an integrated map of female immunity.

By connecting longitudinal peripheral immune profiling with reproductive-tissue biology, we aim to understand how female immune states are coordinated across physiological states and biological compartments.

CONNECTED RESEARCH

Extending the map.

TransMic / TransInf

Immune variation across metabolic and environmental transitions.

Population systems immunology examining how nutrition, metabolism, microbiome and environment shape human immune states and sex-associated variation.

InformatYX

Connecting physiology through computation.

A developing computational framework for integrating hormonal, metabolic, demographic and population context into systems-level immune analysis.
