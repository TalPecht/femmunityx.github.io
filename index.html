Research vision

Immunity in physiological context.

Female immune biology emerges from interacting systems—not from a single variable.

FemmunityX studies immune variation together with endocrine state, metabolism, environmental context and time, linking longitudinal changes in circulation with biology at the reproductive tissue interface.

The direction

Towards an integrated map of female immunity.

Our goal is to connect immune states across physiological conditions, biological compartments and populations—moving from observation of variation toward an integrated systems-level understanding of female immune biology.

Connected research

Extending the map.

Femmunity is one part of a broader research program investigating the biological and computational dimensions of human immune variation.

TransMic / TransInf

Immune variation across metabolic and environmental transitions

Population systems immunology examining how nutrition, metabolism, microbiome and environment shape immune states and sex-associated variation.

InformatYX

Connecting physiology through computation

A computational layer for integrating hormonal, metabolic, demographic and population context into systems-level immune analysis.
